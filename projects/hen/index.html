02 / Engineering problem

Turn product requirements into repeatable hardware.

The engineering challenge is not only creating geometry that functions. A product must also be manufacturable, testable, serviceable, documented, and consistent across suppliers and production.

  • Mechanical performanceGeometry, interfaces, loads, flow path, sealing, and wear.
  • ValidationClear methods, instrumentation, acceptance criteria, and reports.
  • ManufacturingProcess limits, tooling, tolerances, inspection, and supplier capability.
  • Release qualityAccurate drawings, specifications, revisions, and production documentation.
