About

From CAD to physical hardware.

I am a hands-on mechanical engineer who enjoys carrying a design beyond the screen—into prototypes, fixtures, test setups, manufacturing decisions, and real-world troubleshooting.

My strongest work combines structured engineering with practical build experience. I care about clear drawings, measurable requirements, serviceable hardware, and learning from what the test or failed part actually shows.
