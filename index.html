Firefighting Nozzle Product Development & Validation • 2025–present

Problem: Develop and release rugged flow-control products with repeatable performance, clear acceptance criteria, and production-ready documentation.

Solution: Build NFPA 1964 test plans and reports; support pressure/flow instrumentation, Arduino-based cycle testing, supplier QC, injection-molding RFQs, drawing updates, thread research, coating evaluation, and flow-path improvements.

Result: A more structured path from prototype to validated hardware, with test evidence, supplier feedback, and manufacturing requirements captured for release.

Engineering Helper • 2026–present

Problem: Everyday mechanical-design references and calculators are scattered across websites, handbooks, spreadsheets, and notes.

Solution: Created a focused web tool that combines unit conversion, regular/scientific/graphing calculators, threads and drill sizes, fits, materials, section properties, beam bending, O-ring guidance, checklists, and a grid-paper engineering notebook.

Result: One fast workspace for common design checks, with simple and advanced modes and an iterative roadmap driven by real engineering use.

KTM 990 Duke R — Track Setup & Reliability • 2026–present

Problem: Prepare a high-performance street bike for track use while keeping maintenance, diagnostics, and upgrades practical and reliable.

Solution: Developed a service and setup plan covering tires, brakes, suspension, cooling, protective hardware, electrical diagnosis, and evidence-based aftermarket-part selection.

Result: A documented street/track platform with clearer service decisions, repeatable setup changes, and reliability prioritized ahead of cosmetic upgrades.

2005 SV650 Track Build • 2026–present

Problem: Recommission a five-year-stored, track-only SV650 with GSX-R suspension components as a dependable, low-cost first race bike.

Solution: Restored the fuel system, resolved fault codes, selected the battery and 520 drivetrain, refreshed controls and fluids, planned a simplified harness, and evaluated braking, quickshifter, lap-timing, and rider-fit upgrades by value.

Result: The bike now primes and runs, with a staged build plan that puts safety, track time, serviceability, and rider confidence first.

1998 Toyota 4Runner — Fabrication & Overland Systems • 2026–present

Problem: Turn a high-mileage 4Runner into a durable daily driver and capable overland/tow platform without adding unnecessary weight or complexity.

Solution: Rebuilt major suspension and steering wear items; planned brake, cooling, transmission-temperature, dual-battery, roof-rack, armor, drawer, lighting, and trailer-support systems around field repairability.

Result: A phased vehicle-development plan that balances reliability, fabrication effort, cost, payload, and real off-road use.

Battery Press & Automation System (LLNL) • 2024–2025

Problem: Assemble and test experimental batteries under controlled pressure and temperature with repeatability and safety.

Solution: Designed and built a precision press, then supported integration with Python/LabVIEW rigs, instrument control, logging, QC checks, and safety interlocks.

Result: Improved repeatability, traceability, and operator handoff through calibrated hardware, automated analysis, and documented procedures.
